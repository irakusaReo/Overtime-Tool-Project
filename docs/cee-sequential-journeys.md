# CEE journeys, step by step

Every journey starts on the CEE Dashboard and runs in order to its outcome. Blocked routes and loading, empty and error states are journeys of their own, not branches. Each step names what the CEE does and the screen they land on (file in `pages/`).

41 journeys across 9 areas, 174 steps. All 71 screens appear at least once. Generated by `tools/sequential_journeys.py`.
